How your preparation runs

The same rhythm every week, so nothing piles up before the exam.

Every day

Class and assignment

Each class ends with an assignment on what was taught. You finish it the same day, in the study hall, with mentors around to help.

Every week

A 60-mark test

A written test on the week's chapters, evaluated with a marking scheme and suggested answers so you see exactly where marks were lost.

Before the exam

Full mock series

Full-length papers under exam conditions, followed by one-to-one review with your mentor and a revision plan for the last weeks.

What students say

Questions parents and students ask

Where is The Mentors Edge?

Our residential campus is in Kerala. The Contact page has a Google Maps link and directions.

Do you teach CA Final classes?

Classroom teaching is for CA Inter. CA Final students get mentoring, test series, revision support and full use of the residential campus.

Is there a hostel?

Yes. Students can stay on campus, with AC classrooms, study cubicles and discussion rooms open from 7:30 AM to 10:00 PM every day.

Can I join online?

Yes. Online students get the same classes, assignments and weekly tests, evaluated by the same mentors.

How are tests evaluated?

Every weekly test is checked against a marking scheme. You get your marks for each question, suggested answers, and comments on what to improve.

How do I apply?

Fill in the admission form or message us on WhatsApp at +91 90727 10717. We will tell you about the next batch, seats and fees.

Admissions are open

Seats in each batch are limited. Ask us about the next batch and fees.
